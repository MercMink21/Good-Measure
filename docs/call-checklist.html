Discovery Call Checklist

What to cover on every Good Measure client call

A working script, not a rigid one — the goal is to hang up with everything needed to send an accurate quote and never have to call back with "one more question." Skim it before the call, check things off as they come up naturally in conversation.

"Thanks for reaching out — before we talk pricing, I want to make sure I understand your night. Mind if I ask a few questions?"

Client Info

01

The Basics

  • Full name, phone, and best way to reach themShould already be on the inquiry form — confirm it's current.
  • Event date, and a backup/rain date if it's outdoors
  • Start time and how many hours of bar service
  • Venue — home, rental space, park, backyard, etc., and the address
  • Indoor or outdoor, and the weather backup plan if outdoor
  • Final guest countNot the invite list — how many people are actually confirmed. This is what the quote is built on.
  • Which package fits — Intimate Gathering (15 or fewer) or Small Parties
02

The Drink

  • What alcohol are they providing? Ask for types, not just "some liquor" — spirits, wine, beer, brands if they knowYou're BYOB — this determines what's actually possible to make.
  • Flavor profile: sweet, bitter, citrusy, boozy-forward, light and easy?
  • Anything they specifically dislike (won't drink gin, hates anything too sweet, etc.)
  • Is there a drink they want recreated — a favorite from a bar, a wedding cocktail, a family recipe?
  • How many guests are sober / zero-proof?Determines whether they need one N/A option or a full mocktail menu.
  • Any allergies or dietary notes (nuts, dairy, specific spirits)
03

Venue & Logistics

  • Where will the bar actually be set up — counter, table, outdoor cart?
  • Glassware: do they have enough for the guest count and drink type?Client provides glassware — this is the single most common gap to catch before event day, not during it.
  • Power outlet nearby if you'll need one
  • Parking and load-in access for setup
  • Ice — do they have a source, or are you sourcing/bringing it?
  • Trash/recycling plan for the night
04

Add-Ons

  • Interested in custom event signage? (from $20)
  • Any other special touches — a named drink for the guest of honor, a welcome tray, extra garnish requests
05

Budget & Next Steps

  • Any budget range they've mentioned, or ask directly if it hasn't come up
  • Walk through what's included vs. what's an add-on, in plain terms
  • Tell them when they'll receive the written quote
  • Explain deposit/booking process and when the date is actually locked

Before you hang up

Recap the event back to them in one or two sentences — date, guest count, the drink direction — so any mismatch surfaces now, not on quote day. Confirm exactly when they'll hear from you next, and point them to @goodmeasurebar on Instagram if they want to see past events while they wait.
