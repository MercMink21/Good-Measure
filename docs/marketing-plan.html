Marketing Plan

Good Measure — Getting From Live Site to Booked Calendar

A launch-and-grow plan built around what's actually true about the business right now: two packages (Intimate Gathering, Small Parties), BYOB, a 30-mile radius around Colorado Springs, and Instagram as the one social channel. Nothing here assumes a budget or team you don't have.

Positioning

What makes Good Measure different, said plainly

Most private bartending in this market is priced and staffed for weddings. Good Measure is built for the room that stays small on purpose — and that's the pitch, not an apology.

Small by design

15-guest dinner parties and engagement/shower-sized events get the same craft and setup as a 150-person wedding, at a scale and price that fits them.

BYOB, without the awkwardness

Clients keep control of their alcohol budget and brand preferences; Good Measure handles everything else — the part people don't want to think about.

Zero-proof as a real menu, not an asterisk

Most competitors treat mocktails as an afterthought. A full, matching zero-proof program is a genuine differentiator for mixed-dry and sober-curious guest lists.

A custom drink, not a fixed menu

Every package is built around a drink designed with the host — the pitch is "your own recipe," not "pick from our list."

Who You're Talking To

Four segments, all within ~30 miles of 80920

  • Dinner-party hosts — small get-togethers, 15 guests or fewer, want one great drink done right
  • Engagement & shower hosts — want the event to feel special without full-wedding cost or scale
  • Mixed-dry gatherings — hosts who need a real mocktail program, not a lone virgin option
  • Referral-driven repeat hosts — past guests who host their own small events later and remember the bar
Channels

Where the effort actually goes

Ranked by expected return for a one-person, pre-revenue-marketing-budget business. Instagram is the hub; everything else feeds it or feeds off it.

01

Instagram

The only social channel right now (@goodmeasurebar) — so it has to carry both discovery and proof-of-work. Reels get found by strangers; feed posts and highlights convert people who already found you.

  • Content pillars: behind-the-bar process reels, the signature-drink reveal for each event, event recaps, quick "ask a bartender" Q&A in Stories
  • Post 3–4x/week in-season (spring through early fall + holiday season), 1–2x/week off-season
  • Geotag Colorado Springs plus Monument, Woodland Park, Fountain, Black Forest to cover the full 30-mile radius
  • Bio and pinned post lead with the two real differentiators: BYOB and a full zero-proof menu
  • Every booked event ends with a request: a Story repost from the host, or permission to post recap photos
02

Google Business Profile

Free, and it's how people search "private bartender near me" or "bartending service Colorado Springs" instead of scrolling Instagram. Set once, then just feed it reviews.

  • Category: Bartending Service; service area set to the 30-mile radius, not a storefront address
  • Add the logo, 5–10 real event photos once available, and a link straight to the Inquiry section
  • Ask every client for a Google review within a few days of the event — this is the single highest-leverage local SEO action available
03

Local vendor & referral network

Event planners, photographers, florists, and small venue/rental spaces in the same niche (small, non-wedding-scale events) see the exact clients Good Measure wants, before those clients start searching themselves.

  • Direct outreach to 5–10 local planners/photographers with a one-page intro and the Instagram link
  • Simple referral credit (e.g. $25–50 off, or a free add-on) for any booking that names a referrer — vendor or past client
  • Post in 2–3 relevant local Facebook groups (Colorado Springs moms/parenting groups, local events groups) when a booking-friendly moment fits — not cold self-promotion
04

Paid social (later, small)

Worth testing only after organic content and the Google listing are running — paid spend works better once there's real proof (photos, reviews) to show.

  • Small geo-targeted Instagram/Meta test ($100–200) once real event photography exists
  • Target by interest (event planning, home entertaining, engagement) and location (80920 + surrounding zips), not broad demographics
Launch Sequence

First 90 days

WindowFocusConcrete actions
Days 1–14 Soft launch Announce to personal network; book 2–3 discounted "founding client" events in exchange for real photos + a testimonial + a Google review. This is what unblocks the site's remaining placeholders.
Days 15–30 Foundation Claim & complete Google Business Profile. Start the Instagram content cadence. Reach out to 5 local vendors/planners. Wire the inquiry form to Formspree so leads actually land somewhere.
Days 31–60 Proof Replace every placeholder on the site (gallery, testimonials, pricing) with real material from the founding events. Keep posting recaps. First referral-credit bookings should start showing up.
Days 61–90 Push Review what actually drove inquiries (ask every new lead "how'd you hear about us") and put more effort there. Consider the first small paid-ad test if organic is converting well.
The through-line: every one of the first handful of events is also a content and proof-gathering opportunity. Treat the founding-client discount as a marketing spend, not a discount.
