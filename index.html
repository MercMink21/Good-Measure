"Good measure" isn't just a pour — it's a way of hosting. Generous, precise, and never an afterthought.

We started Good Measure because the best parties we'd ever bartended weren't the biggest ones — they were the small dinners, backyard engagements, and twelve-person birthdays where the host actually got to enjoy their own night instead of restocking ice.

Every event starts with a conversation, not a package. You supply the alcohol; we build a short, considered menu around it — plus a full zero-proof menu for anyone (or everyone) skipping the alcohol. Then we show up early, set up a real bar with full glassware and garnish, and stay until the last glass is poured.

No flair, no shouting over the crowd. Just craft cocktails, careful hospitality, and good measure in every sense.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

Do you provide the alcohol?

No — you supply the alcohol for your event. We bring everything else: sodas, club soda, limes, lemons, cups, straws, napkins, ice, and full bar setup.

What's included in every booking?

Full bar setup and clean-up, 2 sodas of your choice, club soda, limes, lemons, plastic cups, cocktail straws, napkins, and ice. Add-ons — including custom event signage from $20 — are available on every package.

How far in advance should we book?

2–4 weeks for weeknight or smaller events, 6+ weeks for weekend celebrations and peak season (May–September, plus the holidays).

Can we do a mocktail-only event?

Yes — full zero-proof menus are a regular part of what we do, built with the same care, garnish, and glassware as a spirited bar.
