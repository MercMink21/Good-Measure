"Good measure" isn't just a pour — it's a way of hosting. Generous, precise, and never an afterthought.

Good Measure was started because the best parties we'd ever attended weren't the biggest ones — they were the small dinners, backyard engagements, and twelve-person birthdays where the host actually got to enjoy their own night instead of restocking ice.

Every event starts with a conversation, not a package. You supply the alcohol and glassware; we build a short, considered menu and bring everything else — plus a full zero-proof menu for anyone (or everyone) skipping the alcohol. Then we show up early, set up the bar, and stay until the last glass is poured.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

Do you provide the alcohol?

No — you supply the alcohol for your event. We bring everything else: sodas, club soda, tonic water, 12oz plastic cups, straws, garnish, ice, and full bar setup.

What's included in every booking?

Full bar setup and clean-up, two sodas of your choice, club soda, tonic water, 12oz plastic cups, cocktail straws, ice, and two garnishes of choice. You'll need to provide the alcohol and glassware. Add-ons — an extra signature cocktail ($50), an extra hour ($75), and custom event signage (from $20) — are available on every package.

How far in advance should we book?

2–4 weeks for weeknight or smaller events, 6+ weeks for weekend celebrations and peak season (May–September, plus the holidays).

Can we do a mocktail-only event?

Yes — full zero-proof menus are a regular part of what we do, built with the same care and garnish as a spirited bar.

What's your service area?

Within 30 miles of Colorado Springs, CO. Travel fees may apply beyond that.
